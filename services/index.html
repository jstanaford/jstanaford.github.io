FREELANCE + CONSULTING

Services

I take on freelance projects and one-off consulting engagements. Below are the shapes of work I'm set up to deliver. If your project doesn't fit one of these cleanly, that's fine. Reach out and we'll figure out the right scope together.

What I Build

05 SHAPES OF WORK
T0 · STATIC LAUNCH SITE
A small, fast, done-in-a-week site.
~ 5 business days

Three to five static HTML/CSS/JS pages, mobile-responsive, basic SEO, contact form, hosted on your Netlify or Cloudflare Pages account. You supply content and images; I build, deploy, and hand you the keys. Best for freelancers, artists, and small operators who need a simple, professional online presence.

T1 · BUSINESS WEBSITE
A more substantial site that supports a real business.
~ 2 weeks

Bespoke layout, richer content architecture, analytics setup, richer forms, more discovery and revision rounds, deployment I manage for you. Best if you want a professional business site but don't already know exactly what it should look like.

T2A · CMS / WORDPRESS
A site you can update yourself, with the plumbing done right.
~ 4 to 6 weeks

WordPress or comparable CMS, custom theme aligned to your brand, admin training so you can edit content yourself, one integration (payment, email, shipping, or similar). Custom post types and taxonomies where they earn their keep. Best if you want to keep adding pages and content after launch without needing a developer every time.

T2B · E-COMMERCE
An online store that actually processes payments and ships.
~ 4 to 8 weeks

Shopify or comparable e-commerce platform. Custom theme, product catalog, checkout configuration, and one custom integration (payment gateway, shipping API, tax service, or similar). I've shipped multiple Laravel/Lunar and WooCommerce stores in past work; happy to talk through what fits your operation.

T3 · SENIOR CONSULTING
Ad hoc engineering help for teams that need it.
Hourly

Cloud architecture reviews, DevOps and CI/CD remediation, production incident investigation, legacy PHP or .NET rescue, AI-assisted engineering workflow implementation, and other work that doesn't fit a fixed-scope package. Best if you have a specific problem and want a senior engineer's time to solve it.

No published rate card. Every engagement gets a custom quote based on scope, timeline, and fit. Reach out and we'll talk it through.
